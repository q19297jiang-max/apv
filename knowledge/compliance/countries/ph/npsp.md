# Philippines National Payment System (NPS)

## Regulatory Authority

**Authority**: Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP)
**Website**: https://www.bsp.gov.ph/
**Source URL**: https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Regulations/Issuances/Regulations/2018/Inst_1049.pdf

## Overview

BSP Circular No. 1049 establishes the regulatory framework for the National Payment System (NPS), providing oversight of payment systems and services in the Philippines.

## Key Requirements

### 1. Payment System Operator (PSO) Registration
**Source**: https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Regulations/Issuances/Regulations/2018/Inst_1049.pdf (Section 4)

- All payment systems must register with BSP
- Designated payment systems require approval
- Regular reporting requirements

### 2. Operational Risk Management
**Source**: https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Regulations/Issuances/Regulations/2018/Inst_1049.pdf (Section 6)

- Robust risk management framework
- Business continuity planning
- Disaster recovery capabilities
- Regular testing

### 3. Technology Standards
**Source**: https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Regulations/Issuances/Regulations/2018/Inst_1049.pdf (Section 7)

- Secure payment processing
- PCI-DSS compliance for card payments
- Encryption standards
- Access controls

### 4. Consumer Protection
**Source**: https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Regulations/Issuances/Regulations/2018/Inst_1049.pdf (Section 8)

- Transparent disclosure
- Fair treatment
- Complaint handling
- Liability frameworks
