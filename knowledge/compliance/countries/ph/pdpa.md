# Philippines Data Privacy Act (DPA)

## Regulatory Authority

**Authority**: National Privacy Commission (NPC)
**Website**: https://privacy.gov.ph/
**Source URL**: https://privacy.gov.ph/data-privacy-act/

## Overview

Republic Act No. 10173, known as the Data Privacy Act of 2012, protects personal data in information and communications systems in both government and private sectors.

## Key Requirements

### 1. General Data Privacy Principles
**Source**: https://privacy.gov.ph/data-privacy-act/ (Section 2)

- Transparency
- Legitimate Purpose
- Proportionality
- Accountability

### 2. Consent
**Source**: https://privacy.gov.ph/data-privacy-act/ (Section 13)

- Explicit consent required for processing personal data
- Consent must be informed and specific
- Right to withdraw consent

### 3. Rights of Data Subjects
**Source**: https://privacy.gov.ph/data-privacy-act/ (Section 16)

- Right to be informed
- Right to access
- Right to object
- Right to erasure/blocking
- Right to damages
- Right to file a complaint
- Right to rectification
- Right to data portability

### 4. Security of Personal Data
**Source**: https://privacy.gov.ph/data-privacy-act/ (Section 20)

- Organizational, physical, and technical security measures
- Regular security assessments
- Data breach notification
