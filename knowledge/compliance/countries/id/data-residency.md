# Indonesia Data Residency Requirements

## Regulatory Authority

**Authority**: Ministry of Communication and Informatics (Kominfo)
**Website**: https://www.kominfo.go.id/
**Source URL**: https://kominfo.go.id/en/contents/peraturan/detail/12200

## Overview

Government Regulation No. 71 of 2019 on Electronic Systems and Transactions establishes data localization requirements for certain categories of data.

## Key Requirements

### 1. Data Localization
**Source**: https://kominfo.go.id/en/contents/peraturan/detail/12200 (Article 14)

- Certain public service data must be stored in Indonesia
- Data controllers must have servers in Indonesia
- Cross-border data transfer restrictions

### 2. Data Categories
**Source**: https://kominfo.go.id/en/contents/peraturan/detail/12200

- Public service data
- Financial transaction data
- Health data
- Education data

### 3. Cross-Border Transfer
**Source**: https://kominfo.go.id/en/contents/peraturan/detail/12200

- Approval required for certain transfers
- Adequate level of protection required
- Contractual requirements
