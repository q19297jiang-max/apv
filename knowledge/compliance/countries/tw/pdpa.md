# Taiwan Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA)

## Regulatory Authority

**Authority**: Ministry of Justice (MOJ)
**Website**: https://www.moj.gov.tw/
**Source URL**: https://www.moj.gov.tw/mp001.html

## Overview

The Personal Data Protection Act governs the collection, processing, and use of personal data in Taiwan.

## Key Requirements

### 1. Data Processing Principles
**Source**: https://www.moj.gov.tw/mp001.html

- Specific purpose
- Adequate and relevant
- Not excessive
- Truthful and complete

### 2. Consent Requirements
**Source**: https://www.moj.gov.tw/mp001.html

- Explicit consent required for sensitive data
- Consent must be informed
- Right to withdraw consent

### 3. Data Subject Rights
**Source**: https://www.moj.gov.tw/mp001.html

- Right to query or request review
- Right to request补充 or correction
- Right to cease collection/processing/use
- Right to erase

### 4. Security Requirements
**Source**: https://www.moj.gov.tw/mp001.html

- Reasonable security measures
- Prevent data from being stolen, leaked, altered, or damaged
- Regular security assessments
