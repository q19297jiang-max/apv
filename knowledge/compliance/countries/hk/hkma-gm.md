# Hong Kong HKMA Technology Risk Management

## Regulatory Authority

**Authority**: Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA)
**Website**: https://www.hkma.gov.hk/
**Source URL**: https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/key-functions/banking-stability/tech-risk-management/

## Overview

The HKMA Technology Risk Management Guidelines provide a framework for authorized institutions to manage technology and cybersecurity risks.

## Key Requirements

### 1. Governance and Oversight
**Source**: https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/key-functions/banking-stability/tech-risk-management/

- Board responsibility for technology risk
- Senior management accountability
- Three lines of defense

### 2. Technology Risk Management
**Source**: https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/key-functions/banking-stability/tech-risk-management/

- Risk identification and assessment
- Risk mitigation strategies
- Risk monitoring and reporting

### 3. Information Security
**Source**: https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/key-functions/banking-stability/tech-risk-management/

- Information security program
- Data protection
- Access control
- Encryption requirements

### 4. Business Continuity
**Source**: https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/key-functions/banking-stability/tech-risk-management/

- BCP and DRP requirements
- Regular testing
- RTO/RPO standards

### 5. Third-Party Risk Management
**Source**: https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/key-functions/banking-stability/tech-risk-management/

- Due diligence on service providers
- Contractual requirements
- Ongoing monitoring

### 6. Cyber Resilience
**Source**: https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/key-functions/banking-stability/tech-risk-management/

- Cyber resilience framework
- Threat and vulnerability management
- Incident management
