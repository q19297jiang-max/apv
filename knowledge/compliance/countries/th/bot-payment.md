# Thailand BOT Payment Systems Regulation

## Regulatory Authority

**Authority**: Bank of Thailand (BOT)
**Website**: https://www.bot.or.th/
**Source URL**: https://www.bot.or.th/English/PaymentSystems/Pages/default.aspx

## Overview

The Payment Systems Act B.E. 2560 (2017) provides the regulatory framework for payment systems in Thailand.

## Key Requirements

### 1. Payment System Operator (PSO) Licensing
**Source**: https://www.bot.or.th/English/PaymentSystems/Pages/default.aspx

- License required for PSOs
- Capital requirements
- Fit and proper tests

### 2. Risk Management
**Source**: https://www.bot.or.th/English/PaymentSystems/Pages/default.aspx

- Technology risk management
- Operational risk controls
- Business continuity planning

### 3. Security Standards
**Source**: https://www.bot.or.th/English/PaymentSystems/Pages/default.aspx

- PCI-DSS compliance
- Encryption requirements
- Access controls
