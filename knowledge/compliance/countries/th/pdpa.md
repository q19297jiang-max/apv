# Thailand Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA)

## Regulatory Authority

**Authority**: Office of the Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC)
**Website**: https://pdpc.go.th/
**Source URL**: https://pdpc.go.th/en/law/personal-data-protection-act-2019/

## Overview

The Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019) is Thailand's comprehensive data protection law.

## Key Requirements

### 1. Data Protection Principles
**Source**: https://pdpc.go.th/en/law/personal-data-protection-act-2019/

- Lawfulness, fairness, transparency
- Purpose limitation
- Data minimization
- Accuracy
- Storage limitation

### 2. Consent Requirements
**Source**: https://pdpc.go.th/en/law/personal-data-protection-act-2019/

- Explicit consent required for sensitive data
- Consent must be informed and specific
- Right to withdraw consent

### 3. Data Subject Rights
**Source**: https://pdpc.go.th/en/law/personal-data-protection-act-2019/

- Right to access
- Right to rectification
- Right to erasure
- Right to object
- Right to data portability

### 4. Data Breach Notification
**Source**: https://pdpc.go.th/en/law/personal-data-protection-act-2019/

- Notify PDPC within 72 hours
- Notify affected data subjects
