# Thailand Financial Institutions Business Act (FIBA)

## Regulatory Authority

**Authority**: Bank of Thailand (BOT)
**Website**: https://www.bot.or.th/
**Source URL**: https://www.bot.or.th/English/PaymentSystems/Pages/default.aspx

## Overview

The Financial Institutions Business Act B.E. 2551 (2008) regulates financial institutions in Thailand, including their payment operations.

## Key Requirements

### 1. Licensing
**Source**: https://www.bot.or.th/English/PaymentSystems/Pages/default.aspx

- Banking licenses
- Payment service provider licenses
- Capital requirements

### 2. Risk Management
**Source**: https://www.bot.or.th/English/PaymentSystems/Pages/default.aspx

- Comprehensive risk management
- Technology risk controls
- Business continuity planning

### 3. Consumer Protection
**Source**: https://www.bot.or.th/English/PaymentSystems/Pages/default.aspx

- Fair treatment
- Transparent disclosure
- Complaint handling
