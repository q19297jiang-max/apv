# Philippines BSP Technology Risk Management Framework

## Regulatory Authority

**Authority**: Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP)
**Website**: https://www.bsp.gov.ph/
**Source URL**: https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Regulations/Issuances/Circulars/2020/c995.pdf

## Overview

BSP Circular No. 995 establishes the Technology Risk Management Framework for all BSP-supervised financial institutions. It provides comprehensive guidance on managing technology and cybersecurity risks.

## Key Requirements

### 1. Governance
**Source**: https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Regulations/Issuances/Circulars/2020/c995.pdf (Section 3)

- Board responsibility for technology risk oversight
- Executive management accountability
- Technology risk management function
- Three lines of defense

### 2. Technology Risk Management
**Source**: https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Regulations/Issuances/Circulars/2020/c995.pdf (Section 4)

- Risk identification and assessment
- Risk mitigation strategies
- Risk monitoring and reporting
- Risk culture and awareness

### 3. Information Security
**Source**: https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Regulations/Issuances/Circulars/2020/c995.pdf (Section 5)

- Information security program
- Data classification and protection
- Access control and authentication
- Encryption requirements

### 4. Business Continuity
**Source**: https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Regulations/Issuances/Circulars/2020/c995.pdf (Section 6)

- Business Continuity Plan (BCP)
- Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP)
- Business Impact Analysis (BIA)
- Regular testing and drills

### 5. Third-Party Risk Management
**Source**: https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Regulations/Issuances/Circulars/2020/c995.pdf (Section 7)

- Due diligence on service providers
- Contractual requirements
- Ongoing monitoring
- Exit strategies

### 6. Incident Management
**Source**: https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Regulations/Issuances/Circulars/2020/c995.pdf (Section 8)

- Incident response procedures
- Reporting requirements to BSP
- Root cause analysis
- Lessons learned
