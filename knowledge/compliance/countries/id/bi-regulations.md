# Indonesia Bank Indonesia IT Regulations

## Regulatory Authority

**Authority**: Bank Indonesia (BI)
**Website**: https://www.bi.go.id/
**Source URL**: https://www.bi.go.id/en/aturan-perbankan/umum/Pages/Teknologi-Informasi.aspx

## Overview

Bank Indonesia regulations require financial institutions to implement comprehensive information technology governance and risk management frameworks.

## Key Requirements

### 1. IT Governance
**Source**: https://www.bi.go.id/en/aturan-perbankan/umum/Pages/Teknologi-Informasi.aspx

- Board oversight of IT
- IT steering committee
- Clear roles and responsibilities

### 2. IT Risk Management
**Source**: https://www.bi.go.id/en/aturan-perbankan/umum/Pages/Teknologi-Informasi.aspx

- Risk assessment and mitigation
- Security controls
- Regular audits

### 3. Business Continuity
**Source**: https://www.bi.go.id/en/aturan-perbankan/umum/Pages/Teknologi-Informasi.aspx

- BCP and DRP requirements
- Regular testing
- RTO/RPO standards
