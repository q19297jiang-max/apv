# Stage 1: Requirements Review Checklist

## Purpose

Validate that RFP requirements are complete and clear before proceeding with solution design.

## Checklist Items (5 checks, 2 minutes)

### 1.1 Requirements Clarity
- [ ] All requirements are clearly defined
- [ ] Ambiguous requirements are identified for clarification
- [ ] Requirements are measurable

### 1.2 Requirements Completeness
- [ ] Functional requirements complete
- [ ] Non-functional requirements complete
- [ ] Compliance requirements identified

### 1.3 Source URL Requirements
- [ ] Country-specific compliance URLs identified
- [ ] PCI-DSS requirements identified
- [ ] Pricing calculator requirements identified

### 1.4 Feasibility Assessment
- [ ] All requirements are achievable
- [ ] No conflicting requirements identified
- [ ] Timeline is realistic

### 1.5 Documentation Review
- [ ] RFP document is complete
- [ ] Addendums and attachments reviewed
- [ ] Clarification questions documented

## Approval Decision

**Status**: [ ] Pass [ ] Needs Clarification [ ] Reject

**If Pass**: Proceed to Stage 2 (Compliance Review)

**If Needs Clarification**: Submit clarification questions to customer
