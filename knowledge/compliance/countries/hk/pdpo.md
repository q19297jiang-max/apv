# Hong Kong Personal Data Privacy Ordinance (PDPO)

## Regulatory Authority

**Authority**: Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data (PCPD)
**Website**: https://www.pcpd.org.hk/
**Source URL**: https://www.pcpd.org.hk/en/pdpo_ordinance.html

## Overview

The Personal Data Privacy Ordinance (Cap. 486) governs the protection of personal data in Hong Kong.

## Key Requirements

### 1. Data Protection Principles (DPPs)
**Source**: https://www.pcpd.org.hk/en/pdpo_ordinance.html

- DPP1: Purpose and manner of collection
- DPP2: Accuracy and duration of retention
- DPP3: Use of personal data
- DPP4: Security of personal data
- DPP5: Information to be generally available
- DPP6: Access to personal data

### 2. Consent Requirements
**Source**: https://www.pcpd.org.hk/en/pdpo_ordinance.html

- Voluntary and explicit consent
- Informed consent
- Right to withdraw consent

### 3. Data Subject Rights
**Source**: https://www.pcpd.org.hk/en/pdpo_ordinance.html

- Right to access
- Right to correction
- Right to erasure (under DPP2)
- Right to data portability

### 4. Data Protection Officer
**Source**: https://www.pcpd.org.hk/en/pdpo_ordinance.html

- DPO appointment recommended
- Contact information must be publicly available

### 5. Data Breach Notification
**Source**: https://www.pcpd.org.hk/en/pdpo_ordinance.html

- Recommended notification to PCPD
- Notification to affected individuals
