# Indonesia Personal Data Protection Law

## Regulatory Authority

**Authority**: Ministry of Communication and Informatics (Kominfo)
**Website**: https://www.kominfo.go.id/
**Source URL**: https://www.kominfo.go.id/en/content/constitutional-court-no-20puu-xx-2022

## Overview

Law No. 27 of 2022 is Indonesia's comprehensive personal data protection law, establishing requirements for processing personal data.

## Key Requirements

### 1. Data Processing Principles
**Source**: https://www.kominfo.go.id/en/content/constitutional-court-no-20puu-xx-2022

- Lawfulness and fairness
- Purpose limitation
- Data minimization
- Accuracy
- Storage limitation

### 2. Data Subject Rights
**Source**: https://www.kominfo.go.id/en/content/constitutional-court-no-20puu-xx-2022

- Right to access
- Right to rectification
- Right to erasure
- Right to object
- Right to data portability

### 3. Data Controller Responsibilities
**Source**: https://www.kominfo.go.id/en/content/constitutional-court-no-20puu-xx-2022

- Consent management
- Data breach notification
- Data protection impact assessment
- Data protection officer
