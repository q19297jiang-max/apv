# Malaysia Financial Services Act (FSA)

## Regulatory Authority

**Authority**: Bank Negara Malaysia (BNM)
**Website**: https://www.bnm.gov.my/
**Source URL**: https://www.bnm.gov.my/financial-services-act-2013

## Overview

The Financial Services Act 2013 (Act 758) is Malaysia's primary legislation regulating financial institutions and financial services. It provides a comprehensive regulatory framework for banking, insurance, and payment services.

## Key Requirements

### 1. Licensing Regime
**Source**: https://www.bnm.gov.my/financial-services-act-2013 (Part II)

**License Types**:
- **Banking License**: Commercial banks, Islamic banks
- **Investment Bank License**: Investment banking services
- **Payment Institution License**: Payment services
- **Money Services Business License**: Remittance, currency exchange

### 2. Capital Requirements
**Source**: https://www.bnm.gov.my/financial-services-act-2013 (Section 38)

- Minimum capital requirements for license holders
- Capital adequacy requirements (Basel III)
- Financial resource maintenance
- Regular capital adequacy reporting

### 3. Risk Management
**Source**: https://www.bnm.gov.my/financial-services-act-2013 (Section 45)

- Implement robust risk management framework
- Technology and cybersecurity risk management
- Operational risk management
- Market risk management
- Credit risk management

### 4. Governance Requirements
**Source**: https://www.bnm.gov.my/financial-services-act-2013 (Section 48)

- Board of Directors composition and responsibilities
- Senior management accountability
- Fit and proper requirements
- Regular board and committee meetings

### 5. Consumer Protection
**Source**: https://www.bnm.gov.my/financial-services-act-2013 (Section 54)

- Fair treatment of customers
- Transparent disclosure of terms and conditions
- Effective complaint handling
- Financial education and awareness

### 6. Technology and Operations
**Source**: https://www.bnm.gov.my/financial-services-act-2013 (Section 58)

- Implement secure technology infrastructure
- Comply with technology risk management guidelines
- Regular security testing and audits
- Business continuity and disaster recovery planning

### 7. Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Countering Financing of Terrorism (CFT)
**Source**: https://www.bnm.gov.my/financial-services-act-2013 (Section 62)

- Implement AML/CFT controls and processes
- Customer due diligence
- Transaction monitoring
- Suspicious transaction reporting

### 8. Reporting and Disclosure
**Source**: https://www.bnm.gov.my/financial-services-act-2013 (Section 66)

- Periodic regulatory returns to BNM
- Annual audited financial statements
- Disclosure of material information
- Prudential reporting
