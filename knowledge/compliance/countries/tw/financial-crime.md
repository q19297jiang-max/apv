# Taiwan Financial Crime Control Act

## Regulatory Authority

**Authority**: Financial Supervisory Commission (FSC)
**Website**: https://www.fsc.gov.tw/en/home
**Source URL**: https://www.fsc.gov.tw/en/home

## Overview

The Financial Crime Control Act establishes requirements for preventing financial crimes, including money laundering and terrorist financing.

## Key Requirements

### 1. AML/CFT Program
**Source**: https://www.fsc.gov.tw/en/home

- Comprehensive AML/CFT program
- Customer due diligence
- Transaction monitoring

### 2. Reporting Requirements
**Source**: https://www.fsc.gov.tw/en/home

- Suspicious transaction reporting
- Currency transaction reporting
- STR reporting to investigation authorities

### 3. Risk-Based Approach
**Source**: https://www.fsc.gov.tw/en/home

- Risk assessment
- Enhanced due diligence for high-risk customers
- Regular review of risk controls
